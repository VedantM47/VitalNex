PUNE CLINICAL RISK SYNTHETIC DATASET
Version: 1.0
Rows: 20,000
Purpose: Academic Responsible AI / Healthcare AI Impact Assessment project.

IMPORTANT:
This dataset contains NO real patient records and must NOT be represented as real Pune/Maharashtra patient data.
It is synthetic and designed for model-development, explainability, fairness, reliability and governance experiments.

DESIGN BASIS:
1. UCI Heart Disease was used as the structural reference for the core clinical feature set:
   age, sex, cp, trestbps, chol, fbs, restecg, thalach, exang, oldpeak, slope, ca, thal.
   UCI documents 303 instances, 13 predictive features and a heart-disease target. 
2. Indian/Maharashtra health-risk context was informed by government/ICMR sources:
   - ICMR INDIAB reported Maharashtra urban hypertension prevalence of about 30.5% and rural prevalence of about 24.5% in its Phase I study.
   - ICMR identifies hypertension, raised blood glucose, obesity, tobacco use, physical inactivity and unhealthy diet among important NCD risk factors.
   - Maharashtra HMIS on data.gov.in contains district/sub-district indicators including hypertension, stroke, disease and health-system measures.
3. Pune-specific HMIS sources provide local health-system context, but they are aggregated rather than patient-level.
4. Because no verified open patient-level Pune clinical dataset was established for this build, patient-level values and labels here are SYNTHETIC.

TARGET CONSTRUCTION:
heart_disease_risk is generated using a transparent synthetic risk mechanism combining age, sex, BP, cholesterol, diabetes, smoking, family history, BMI, hypertension, exercise-related variables and UCI-like cardiac variables, plus random variation.
It is NOT a validated clinical risk score and must not be used for medical decisions.

RECOMMENDED USE:
- Baseline ML model
- SHAP/LIME explanations
- Fairness analysis across sex, locality_type and socioeconomic_band
- Confidence/calibration experiments
- Data quality and missingness stress tests
- AI impact assessment and governance dashboard
- Human-oversight and mitigation experiments
